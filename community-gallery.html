▸ ABOUT THE ARCHIVE
This is the community archive for PATTERN RETRIEVAL — a generative, onchain NFT art project by @adamilenich. Every printable ASCII character stored in a Hopfield network, shattered into noise, and recalled. Submit anything you have made using or inspired by the project.
▸ SUBMISSION GUIDELINES
All mediums welcome. Original work only. Include your Twitter handle to be credited. Images are stored locally in your browser as a demo — connect a backend (Airtable / Supabase) for persistent community submissions.
